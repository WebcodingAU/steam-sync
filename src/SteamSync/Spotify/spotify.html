Spotify isn't set up yet

This build of Gaming Enjoyer has no Spotify Client ID. It needs one from a Spotify developer app:

  1. Open developer.spotify.com/dashboard and create an app.
  2. Redirect URI: http://127.0.0.1:43819/spotify/callback; APIs: Web API and Web Playback SDK.
  3. Put its Client ID in SpotifyClientId in appsettings.json.

Play Spotify in Gaming Enjoyer

Sign in with your Spotify account in your browser. Gaming Enjoyer then shows up as a device in Spotify Connect and plays your music itself, so the Spotify app can stay closed.

Playing music here needs Spotify Premium (Spotify's rule for players like this one).
