AMDFamily17.bin and IntelMSR.bin are PawnIO modules, unmodified and signed by their author, from the
official PawnIO.Modules release 0.2.11:

    https://github.com/namazso/PawnIO.Modules/releases/tag/0.2.11

They are licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License version 2.1 (COPYING.txt in this folder).
Their source code is at:

    https://github.com/namazso/PawnIO.Modules/tree/0.2.11

The Gaming Enjoyer relay helper loads one of them into PawnIO (https://pawnio.eu) to read the CPU's
temperature, and only when you turn "CPU temperature" on in the Gaming Enjoyer tray menu. PawnIO itself
is installed separately, from its official installer, and can be removed from Apps in Windows Settings.
